Family Purity, the First Expanded Edition

Rabbi Fishel Jacobs’ guide to marital fulfillment, first published in 1998, returns about half again as long.

First published in 1998 and reprinted many times since, Rabbi Fishel Jacobs’ Family Purity: A Guide to Marital Fulfillment has guided couples across the English-speaking world for nearly three decades. The First Expanded Edition gathers what those years of teaching, counseling and answering questions have clarified. It is about half again as long as the original.

At a glance

  • ½ againas long as the original edition
  • 4×the length of the chapter on childbirth
  • Newappendix on gynecology: conditions and procedures

What’s new

The growth is greatest where couples’ questions are most practical. The chapter on childbirth is four times its former length, and now follows pregnancy, labor, delivery, Caesarean birth and miscarriage step by step. The chapters on separation dates and on intervening issues before immersion have more than doubled. Nearly every other chapter has grown too, with guidance on gynecological exams and ultrasounds, dental work and medical devices, and travel across time zones.

A major new appendix on gynecology explains common conditions and procedures in plain language, and how each bears on these laws. A new overview walks through the monthly cycle at a glance, chapters close with a summary, and a new section presents the primary biblical sources. The opening “Counsel from the Rebbe” has been expanded.

The language has been made plainer still. Hebrew terms are kept to a minimum, notes appear on the page, and where other leading authorities differ, the footnotes say so. The new edition carries a fresh approbation from Rabbi Tuvia HaLevi Kasimov, Dayan in Crown Heights, alongside the original approbations.

Like the original, it is neither strict nor lenient: clear, practical and warm, written for newlyweds and for couples throughout married life.

“Our family is the most important thing in our lives. Observing these laws is a gift from Above. Publishing the original edition was a great merit, and it is an even greater joy to now release this first expanded edition.”

Rabbi Fishel Jacobs

When and where

The expanded edition is due out soon. It will be distributed by Judaica World and sold on Amazon. Until then, the current edition is on Amazon, and every chapter is free to read on this site.
