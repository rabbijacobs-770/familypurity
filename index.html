New site, with a blog

For decades I’ve been writing in halacha, book after book. Over the years many rabbonim have shaped my life through their personal relationships with me. This blog shares the more personal, practical, experiential side of my decades in family purity, in the hope it benefits others.

About

Shalom, I’m Rabbi Jacobs

For over four decades, my passion has been strengthening Jewish family values. I have no greater joy than seeing happy couples and happy families.

  • 1992Trained as a rabbinic attorney, helping women in Israeli rabbinical courts obtain a get from husbands who refused.
  • 1998Ordained by the Chief Rabbis of Israel and went on to specialize in the laws of family life.
  • 20 yearsPublished eight acclaimed books on practical Talmudic law, used worldwide.
  • 2009–nowResponding rabbi for leading family purity websites.

This is my purpose: seeing couples grow together and raise wholesome, loving families. Use the free resources here, and contact me anytime. I’m happy to help.
