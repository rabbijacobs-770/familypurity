Glossary

The Hebrew terms used in the books, in plain English.

niddah נדה
The status of a woman from the start of menstruation until she immerses in a mikveh.
hefsek tahara הפסק טהרה
An internal examination before sunset confirming that bleeding has stopped. It can be done from the fifth day.
moch dochuk מוך דחוק
A soft white cloth inserted after the hefsek tahara and removed after nightfall.
seven white days שבעה נקיים
Seven days after the hefsek tahara, with examinations twice daily, white underwear and white sheets.
bedikah בדיקה
An internal examination with a soft white cloth.
mikveh מקוה
A pool of natural water built according to Jewish law, used for immersion.
tevilah טבילה
Immersion in the mikveh, after nightfall following the seventh white day.
chatzitzah חציצה
Anything that would come between the body and the water during immersion.
kesomim כתמים
Stains found on the body, clothing or sheets, rather than on an examination cloth.
onah עונה
A half-day: daytime (sunrise to sunset) or nighttime (sunset to sunrise).
vestos וסתות
The dates when the next period can be expected, on which a couple separates.
yom hachodesh יום החודש
The monthly cycle: the same Hebrew date as the last period began.
onah beinonis עונה בינונית
The average cycle: the 30th day, counting the day the period began as day one.
veses haflagah וסת הפלגה
The interval cycle: the same number of days as the last interval.
Or Zarua אור זרוע
The custom in many communities of separating one extra half-day before each separation date.
choson & kallah חתן וכלה
Groom and bride.
