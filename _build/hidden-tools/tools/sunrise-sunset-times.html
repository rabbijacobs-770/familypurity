How it works

  1. Daytime is from sunrise to sunset; nighttime is from sunset to sunrise. Shulchan Aruch Admur Hazoken 184
  2. The hefsek tahara is done before sunset.
  3. Separation dates are kept in the half of the day (onah) when the last period began.
  4. For nightfall times, such as for immersion, follow your community’s calendar.
Accuracy

Times are calculated on your device and are accurate to within a minute or two.

Common questions

Why doesn’t it show nightfall?

Nightfall (tzeis) is calculated differently by different communities. Use your community’s calendar for it.

Can it use my exact location?

Yes. Tap “Use my location.” Your location stays on your device.
