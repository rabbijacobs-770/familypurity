At what time?

How it works

  1. The day the niddah state began counts as day one, whether it began in the daytime or the night before.
  2. The hefsek tahara can be done from the fifth day, before sunset. Family Purity, Chapter 5; Ramo 196:11
  3. The seven white days begin the day after the hefsek tahara, with examinations twice daily. Chapter 6
  4. Immersion in the mikveh is after nightfall at the end of the seventh day. Overview
Example

Began Sunday → earliest hefsek tahara Thursday before sunset → seven white days Friday to the next Thursday → mikveh Thursday night.

Common questions

What if the hefsek tahara was done later than the fifth day?

Enter its date in the optional field. The seven white days are counted from the day after it.

What if mikveh night falls on Friday night or Yom Tov?

Special preparation rules apply. See Chapter 7 in Family Purity, or ask Rabbi Jacobs.

Does this follow my community’s custom?

It follows the five-day minimum as brought in Family Purity (Ramo 196:11). If your community follows a different custom, ask your rabbi.

Is anything I enter saved or sent?

No. The calculation happens on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
